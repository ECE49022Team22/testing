Deadlines

every stream checked against its budget, every 0.5 s
streambudgetp99 nowworston time
Budgets: IMU interrupt serviced within 10 ms (the BNO085 stops signalling after ~10 ms); IMU sample every 10 ms, late if over 15 ms; LiDAR packet delivered within 10 ms of its best case; GPS fix within 20 ms of its 5 Hz grid; stop sign detected within 150 ms of capture. "On time" = share of 0.5 s windows whose worst sample stayed within budget, since the demo started.
